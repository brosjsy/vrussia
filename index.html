Who can you become in Russia?

Start with a name, a passport and a dream. Survive your first winter, get your papers, build a career — and see how far you can go in two years.

  • 🎯 Pick your start: a Moscow kid, a student from Lagos, a worker from Samarkand or Osh — every life starts hard in its own way.
  • 🏆 Climb: from your first rented room to a flat of your own, from courier to your own business, from newcomer to citizen. Or get deported trying.
  • ⚖️ Every choice counts: papers, money, the law, love and family — the game remembers everything.
  • 🌍 Compete online: the challenge of the day, the leaderboard, the dish of the day. And learn on the way how life in Russia really works: registration, the patent, public services, the language and traditions.
