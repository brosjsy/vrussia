# How In Russia earns money from the people who play

Plan only: nothing in this file is built. It is the second half of [REVENUE.md](REVENUE.md) (which covers grants and organisations). Research was done on 2026-11-01. **Every price, rate and count that I did not find in a source is marked "assumed"** and has to be tested on real players before anyone relies on it.

## 1. The decision this plan makes

The business thesis says "free for the player, no advertising, no paid advantages". To earn from play, this plan changes one word and keeps the rest:

> The whole game, every story and every safety lesson stay **free**. Players may **pay for extras that never change how a story ends or what the law says**.

What stays forbidden (the players are newcomers who are already targets of fraud, and the game teaches them to spot it):

- No paid advantage: no bought money, health, papers, status, or better outcomes.
- No random paid rewards (loot boxes). Every paid item is shown with its price and what it is. Russia has no loot-box law yet, but the Interior Ministry has spoken against them and a law on age limits for in-game spending is being discussed (searches found proposals only), so this keeps the product clear of both.
- No paywall in front of the scam, rights, registration or exam lessons.
- No selling or sharing player data. No commission from services the game points to.
- Purchases for **18 and over** only (the same rule as texts and calls), until a lawyer says otherwise.
- The payment page must be unmistakably ours, and the game must say in plain words that it never asks for card details inside a story and never asks for money by message.

## 3. The offers

Five things a player may buy, in the order they should be built. Prices are **assumed**.

1. **Exam practice pass** (₽349 for 60 days). A bank of practice questions and timed mock exams for the Russian-language, history and law exam, written by us from public sample tasks, with an explanation and a source for each answer. It is the strongest offer: the buyer's own cost of failing is thousands of roubles and a lost week. Before building: check which sample tasks may be reused, and ask a lawyer how to word it (it is practice, not the official exam; the law now bars middlemen from running the exams themselves, which does not touch practice).
2. **Plus** (₽149 a month or ₽990 a year, assumed). Convenience, not advantage: a printable real-life checklist and progress report (PDF, in the player's language), unlimited saved runs and a story history, an offline pack, the full set of looks (below). Cancel online in one step; no automatic renewal from a removed card (the law above).
3. **Looks** (₽49 to ₽149 each, ₽299 a bundle). Phone skins beyond the free ones, outfits, flat styles, paint colours and sign styles in the land and shop designer, fancy-looking (never real) phone numbers. Purely visual.
4. **A seat for someone else** (₽990, a code). Relatives abroad, employers or the player's friends pay for another person. The people who earn the money are often not the people who need the lessons, so this reaches the buyer who has a card. Employers can buy a block of codes (this is the bridge to the organisation sales in `REVENUE.md`).
5. **Optional support** (a "thank you" button, any amount). Small; not planned on.

**Lagos Life, the closest case, earned mostly from real advertisers on in-game billboards, not from players; what that means for us is in [REVENUE_LAGOS_LIFE.md](REVENUE_LAGOS_LIFE.md).** In short: it does not pay at our size, and the useful version is a verified partner's place in the city sold inside the licence.

**Not recommended now: open advertising.** A Yandex Games listing could carry rewarded video, and it is a real option for a separate build, but the thesis promised none, the marking rules for ads inside games were not found, and every ad placed beside a scam lesson risks the game's trust. If the owner wants it: only in a separate Yandex Games build, only opt-in, never in a story, never for finance, loans or remittances. Decide it as its own question.

## 4. How the money would arrive

| Where the buyer is | Route | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| In Russia, with a bank card | Hosted payment page of a Russian provider (ЮKassa or T-Bank), card (Mir, Visa) and the Faster Payments System (SBP) | choose provider, get the real tariff, receipts |
| Anywhere else (Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, before arrival) | Gift codes bought in roubles by a relative, an employer or a partner in Russia | no local route; **out of scope** by the owner's rule below |

**The owner's rule: all money is in roubles only.** So no Uzbek wallets (Payme, Click, Uzum), no Telegram Stars, no dollars. Russian cards and the Faster Payments System, plus gift codes. Anyone abroad gets the paid extras through a code. Real roubles and the game's own invented roubles must never be confused: see [PARTNER_PLACES.md](PARTNER_PLACES.md), section 0.

## 5. What it could earn

Roubles, 24 months. Inputs: registered players from the thesis (5,000 at 12 months, 30,000 at 24); share who ever pay (assumed); spend per payer per year (assumed). Counted once at each year end, which is generous; payment fees and tax are not taken off.

| | Pay | Spend a year | Year 1 | Year 2 | 24 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cautious | 1% | 700 | 50 payers = 35,000 | 300 = 210,000 | **245,000** |
| Base | 2.5% | 900 | 125 = 112,500 | 750 = 675,000 | **787,500** |
| Good | 5% | 1,200 | 250 = 300,000 | 1,500 = 1,800,000 | **2,100,000** |

The honest reading: **at 30,000 players this is pocket money, not a business.** Player income only matters at a much larger reach.

- Scale check 1: 200,000 registered players, 3% paying, ₽900 each = 6,000 payers = **₽5.4 million** a year.
- Scale check 2, from the exam funnel: if the practice pass reached 1% of the 201,675 sittings of one quarter, that is about 2,000 buyers x ₽349 = ₽698,000 a quarter, about **₽2.8 million** a year. The exam market is large (about 477,000 sittings in the two quarters I found), so this is the place to aim.

The size of the audience, not the price, is the lever. Reaching it costs money for acquisition, which I could not estimate; the free ways are university and employer partners handing out the game (which joins this plan to `REVENUE.md`), Telegram channels and groups of each community, and a Yandex Games listing.

## 6. Test before building anything

**Built (2026-11-02): the Extras app on the phone** (`src/extras.ts`, `src/extras-ui.ts`, migration `server/migrations/0012_interest.sql`). It shows the four offers with the planned price in real roubles, says at the top "Not on sale yet. Nothing is charged, nothing is asked of you, nothing about you is stored", and has one button, "I would want this". A press adds 1 to an anonymous server counter (`interest_exam`, `interest_plus`, `interest_looks`, `interest_gift`) and is remembered on that device so it counts once per offer per device. No card, e-mail or account is asked for. The test is shown until `EXTRAS_OPEN_UNTIL` (2027-01-31; change it in `src/extras.ts`). Read the numbers from `GET /api/counters` (the same list as the visit counter) and divide each by `visits`. **Caveat:** counts are rough (a person with two devices counts twice, and anyone can call the counter from a script), so read them as a ranking of offers, not as a forecast.

1. **Fake door** (2 weeks). Show the offers as buttons with prices to the next 1,000 players: "Exam practice, ₽349", "Plus, ₽149 a month", "Gift a seat". A click opens "Not open yet: leave your e-mail". Count clicks per offer. **Build an offer only if at least 2% of players click it.** Be honest on the screen that it is not on sale yet.
2. **Ten interviews** with players from three countries about what they would pay for and who would pay for them (who holds the card).
3. **A small exam pass by hand**: 50 questions, sold for ₽349 by bank transfer to 20 people. If fewer than 5 buy, rethink the price or the offer.
4. **Price test** on Plus: ₽99, ₽149, ₽199 shown to equal groups.

## 7. What must be built (after a test passes)

Not started. For the owner's planning:

- Server: a purchases table with an idempotent payment webhook (checks the provider's signature), entitlements per account, refunds, gift codes; tests for each. The server already has accounts, an adult flag and rate limits to build on.
- Game: a shop screen (visible price, what it is, no random items), the entitlements checked in the client, the exam question bank as a content pack (the packs system already exists), the PDF report and checklist.
- Money: a legal entity or self-employed status, a payment provider contract, receipts, a refund and complaints page, terms of sale in Russian and English, and the privacy policy updated for payments (`src/platform/policy.ts`).
- Trust: a "we never ask for money in a story or by message" note on the purchase page and in the scam lessons.

## 8. Risks

- **Too few players:** the main risk (section 5). Mitigation: partners distribute the game.
- **Players cannot pay:** no card, no local route. Mitigation: gift codes, and the seat offer.
- **Seen as exploiting a vulnerable group:** the rules in section 1 are the defence; keep them public in the terms.
- **Law changes:** subscriptions (376-FZ), loot boxes, age limits on spending, the exam rules. A lawyer's read before launch.
- **Exam content rights and accuracy:** the exam rules changed in July 2025; wrong practice answers would harm players. Every answer needs a source and a review date, as the rest of the game has.
- **Store dependence:** staying on our own site with our own provider avoids it, at the cost of discovery.
