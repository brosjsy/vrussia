reg_first
 T: Registration — the clock is ticking
 X: The rules give a short time to register your place of stay after arrival: 7 working days for most visitors; for people who come to work, 15 days for citizens of Tajikistan and Uzbekistan and 30 days for those of Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan and Armenia (Belarus: 90). As reported in 2024; the rules change often and the game simplifies how the days are counted. Your host, a landlord or a hostel has to submit the notice through the MFC, post office or Gosuslugi.
 1V Ask your landlord to submit the notice (honest)
 2V Buy "registration" from a fixer for ₽5,000
 3F Postpone => The clock keeps ticking.

 X: A work patent is paid for in monthly advance payments of income tax. In Moscow it was ₽8,900 a month in 2025 and rose to about ₽10,000 from 1 January 2026 (other regions differ).
