# «В России» — the player's guide (how to play every part)

This guide explains how to play, step by step. The game is in English and Russian (the **RU** button on the title screen).
Keyboard everywhere: **1–9** choose an answer, **Enter** continues, **Esc** closes a window.

---

## 1. Starting a life

1. Open the game and press **New game**.
2. **Choose who you are.** Each start has its own rules, money and risks:
   - a rich Moscow kid;
   - a young person from Saratov;
   - a Tajik, Uzbek or Kyrgyz worker;
   - a foreign student from Lagos (in Kazan);
   - a student from Dagestan.
3. **Choose the difficulty:** Easy, Normal or Realistic. Higher difficulty means less money, more police checks and dearer living.
4. **Make your character:** skin, height, hair, face, clothes and what you dream of becoming. The ready-made characters are a quick start.
5. **Foreigners** can tick **start at home**. You then go through the papers before you travel:
   - the invitation, tests and insurance;
   - the visa form (find the mistakes) and the consulate appointment and interview;
   - the ticket, and the migration card at the border.

   Visa-free countries skip the visa.
6. You arrive in your city. The **first-month checklist** says what matters first.

**The goal:** live two years (730 days) and become who you want to be. A life also ends earlier if you are deported, run out of money or your health gives out.

## 2. A day

- Each day has three parts: **morning, afternoon and evening**. Every action takes one part. Small things at home (tea, a shower, sitting down) take none.
- **Needs fall during the day:** hunger, hygiene, social contact and fun. Keep them up, or stress grows and health suffers.
- **Rent is paid on Saturdays.** Utilities (if you rented through the Home app) are billed each month.
- **Scenes** come up with 2–4 choices. Characters remember what you chose. A choice marked **(N ₽)** is greyed out when you cannot pay.

## 3. Papers and the law

- **Your documents are in your profile:**
  - migration registration and the medical exam;
  - health insurance;
  - the work patent and its monthly payment;
  - the language test, RVP, VNZh and citizenship, with the real state fees.
- **The game warns you before a paper expires.** Expired papers bring police checks, fines and legal strikes. **Three strikes mean deportation.**
- **The Police and law app** on the phone explains crimes and fines. Calling **102** is always an option.

## 4. Where you live: your flat and your address

- **Your home depends on who you are**, and you can change it:

  | Home | Who lives there |
  |---|---|
  | A bunk in a shared flat | workers who have just arrived |
  | A room in a block-type dormitory | students |
  | A flat in a panel block | the regional family |
  | A roomy flat in a stalinka | the Moscow family |

- **Rent another home:** open the **Home** app on the phone. The choices are a room in a **kommunalka**, a **one-room khrushchyovka**, a **studio**, a **euro-two** in a new block, or a two-room flat. You pay two weeks' rent as a deposit.
- **Buy a home** (a studio, a village house) or **build your own house**, stage by stage, from the **Home** tab of **Buy mode**.
- **Every flat has real rooms:** a hallway, a kitchen, a bathroom and rooms, divided by low walls so you see everything. Each type looks like its real-life version:
  - the khrushchyovka has a combined bathroom and a tiny kitchen with a gas stove;
  - the kommunalka has your neighbours' doors and one stove for each family in the shared kitchen;
  - the izba has the Russian stove.
- **Your address:** a real street and district of your city, with your own house, entrance and flat number. It is shown on the blue plate by your entrance, over your flat, and on your online home card. Your window lights up in the evening. *The streets are real; the house and flat numbers are invented.*
- **Inside your flat:** press **Go inside** in the 3D view. Click the bed, wardrobe, fridge, stove, desk, bath or phone, and your character walks there and uses it.
- **Furnishing:** in your flat, **Buy mode** opens about 50 pieces of furniture. Buy one and put it on the floor or the wall; tap it to pick it up. Pieces never block the door, a wall or the way to the things you use.
- **Renovating:** the **Home** tab of Buy mode changes the floor, walls, light and windows. These cost money.

## 5. The 3D city

- The 3D city opens by itself when your device can draw 3D (or press the **3D** button). It is a model of your real city:
  - its river or sea;
  - its typical houses: khrushchyovkas, panel blocks, stalinkas, new towers, old houses with pilasters, wooden houses;
  - its famous sights beyond the edge: the Kremlin and St Basil's in Moscow, Kul Sharif in Kazan, The Motherland Calls in Volgograd, and the bridges of Vladivostok.
- **Click a sight** to learn its name, year and a fact.
- **Click a building** to travel there. Click a person to talk.
- **Buttons:**
  - **Traffic** shows or hides the cars;
  - **City hall** opens the weekly election;
  - the **real map** (optional, it asks first) shows your city's real streets.

### The real city (🌍 Real city)

- Press **🌍 Real city** above the map. The first time, the game asks before it downloads anything from the internet.
- You walk your real city at real scale: real streets, real buildings, and every named place on OpenStreetMap. Around the centre of Moscow that is over 16,000 places: metro stations, churches, mosques, MFC offices, banks, clinics, schools, universities, cafés, shops, museums, parks and stations. The map loads around you as you walk.
- **Find a place:** type in the search box (for example «метро», «храм», «мечеть», «МФЦ», or a café's name), or press a quick button (MFC, Bank, Clinic, University, Groceries, Station) for the nearest one.
- **The place card** shows the real name, what kind of place it is, how far it is and how many minutes on foot.
  - **Go here:** your character walks there along the real streets, with a blue line on the ground and step-by-step directions ("Turn right onto Rabochaya Street, 250 m").
  - **Walk there now:** you arrive at once.
  - **Do something here:** does the game action that fits the place: papers at an MFC, money at a bank, study at a university, a meal at a café, culture at a church or museum.
- **Leave the real city** returns to the game's own map.
- The map data comes from OpenStreetMap. Opening hours are not shown, so check them before a real visit.

## 6. Work, money and study

- **Jobs:**
  - **Jobs app** for starter shifts: courier and barista mini-games.
  - **Job search** action for a permanent job. There are 56 jobs, from courier to the new **information-security analyst (SOC)**.
  - After 8, 24, 56 and 110 good shifts you are promoted.
- **State service** app: police, migration service, MFC, railways and more, each with a workday of real-looking cases.
- **Bank app:** balance, cards, transfers home, loans and savings goals. **Business:** register as self-employed or an individual entrepreneur and grow a company.
- **Study app:**
  - courses including Russian A1–C1, programming and **Cybersecurity**: enrol, attend, do homework at your desk, pass the exam;
  - five Russian words a day.

## 7. How to cook (the Kitchen)

1. Open the **Kitchen** app on the phone, or click the stove in your flat.
2. **Choose a dish.** There are 1,039 dishes from 28 cuisines. You can:
   - search;
   - take a suggestion;
   - type **what you have** (for example "potato, egg, onion") and see what you can make;
   - filter by diet (halal, vegetarian, vegan).
3. **Take the ingredients from the shelf.** Missing ones can be bought at the market (bargain there) or delivered.
4. **Prepare each ingredient safely.** Each step asks a question. For example:
   - raw chicken is not washed, only patted dry and cut on its own board;
   - frozen food is thawed in the fridge;
   - red beans are boiled hard.
5. **Answer the four steps of that kind of dish** (a soup, a pie, a salad…), the **kitchen safety question** and the **doneness check**.
6. **Keep the leftovers properly.** You get stars, and every mistake is explained. A dangerous mistake can give you food poisoning later.
7. **More to do:**
  - cook for guests (watch their allergies);
  - win cuisine medals and rise through the ranks to unlock harder dishes;
  - keep food in the fridge, where it goes off;
  - take the **daily online challenge** (if you are logged in) to see your place on the table.

## 8. City life, transport and people

- **City life:** the **City life** app lists 437 everyday activities (health, sport, culture, faith, friends, children, travel, repairs, holidays in their real months). Each shows its price, time, what it needs and what it gives.
- **Transport:** the metro of the real city (7 cities), buses, trains between the 37 cities, a full flight-booking site, the driving school and a car.
- **People:** friends, dating, marriage at the ZAGS, children, pets, and 14 recurring characters with their own stories.
- **Weather and seasons** change what is safe and pleasant.

## 9. Cyber life: how not to be fooled

- **Scenes from real life:** they come up in ordinary days, and each one explains the trick and the law behind it. You will meet:
  - "your mobile contract expires";
  - "confirm your doctor's appointment";
  - "vote for my niece";
  - a "walking courier" job and "lend me your card" (both are crimes: art. 159 and art. 187 of the Criminal Code);
  - a cloned voice of a relative;
  - a cheap flat with a viewing fee, and a QR code on a parking meter;
  - a password leak, and a phishing letter at work;
  - a loan taken under pressure, which you can cancel during the 4-hour cooling-off period;
  - for foreigners, a SIM card "without papers".
- **The Cyber safety app** (🛡️ on the phone):
  1. **Scam or not?** Five messages a day. Press **Scam** or **It is fine**, and the app explains the sign that gives it away. Right answers raise your level from Beginner to Cyber defender.
  2. **Your protection:** switch on the real tools:
     - the self-ban on loans (Gosuslugi, since 1 March 2025);
     - a trusted person, the "second hand" (banks, since 1 September 2025);
     - two-step login on Gosuslugi;
     - the self-ban on SIM contracts;
     - different passwords;
     - updates from the official store.

     A protection that is on makes the matching trap harmless in the scenes.
  3. **If you have been fooled**, follow these steps:
     - block the card by the number on its back;
     - call 102;
     - change your passwords;
     - never tell anyone a code;
     - wait 24 hours before any "urgent" transfer.
- **A career:** pass the **Cybersecurity** course, join a **CTF** at the university and report bugs through **bug bounty** (never download other people's data). Then work as an information-security analyst.

## 10. Online with other players

- Press **🌐 Online** on the title screen to make an account (e-mail and password; the confirmation link comes by e-mail). You then get:
  - a cloud save and the leaderboard;
  - who is online, and chat at each place;
  - private messages (adults only);
  - friends, and your home card with your address and a guest book;
  - the city vote, adverts on the 3D billboards, weekly co-op challenges and the Kitchen's daily table.
- **To block someone:** open their card and press **Block**. The Friends tab lists the people you blocked, with **Unblock**.
- Phone numbers and messenger names are not allowed in messages or adverts. This keeps everyone safe from scams outside the game.

## 11. Settings, saving and help

- **RU / EN** language, **Aa** text size, sound on or off, saver mode for slow devices.
- The game **saves by itself in your browser**. Logged-in players also have a cloud save. Saves can be exported and imported.
- The **💡 Help** app on the phone and the **first-month checklist** explain what to do next.
