## Laws
- 41-FZ of 01.04.2025 (anti-fraud package 1): ban on informing via foreign messengers (1 Jun 2025); labelled calls from companies, consent for mass calls, ban on passing a number to others, SIM self-ban via Gosuslugi/MFC (lifted only in person at an MFC), SMS during a call delivered after the call, ATM voluntariness checks, "second hand" trusted person (all 1 Sep 2025); GIS "Антифрод" (1 Mar 2026). H
- Trusted person ("second hand"): confirms chosen operations within 12 hours, otherwise the operation is refused; has no access to the money (cbr.ru). H
- SIM limits: citizens 20 numbers (303-FZ; Gosuslugi "Сим-карты" service since 1 Apr 2025); foreigners 10 numbers, in-person contract confirmed via Gosuslugi (ESIA) and biometrics, IMEI in the contract (from 1 Jan 2025). H
- Fines for passing your number to someone (КоАП 13.29.1, from 1 Sep 2025): citizens 30–50 k ₽. M
- 210-FZ of 26.06.2026 ("Антифрод 2.0"): unified card register (1 Sep 2026); limit of 20 cards per person (1 Sep 2027); compensation by banks/operators that failed anti-fraud duties, 6-hour hold of transfers, self-ban on incoming international calls, the Gosuslugi "red button" to report fraud (1 Mar 2027). H/M
- Self-ban on loans: Gosuslugi from 1 Mar 2025, MFC from 1 Sep 2025, free; lifting takes effect on the second calendar day; a loan issued despite the ban need not be repaid (cbr.ru/ckki/self-prohibition_credit/). H
- Cooling-off period (from 1 Sep 2025): loans 50–200 k ₽ paid after 4 hours, over 200 k ₽ after 48 hours. H
- Two-day pause on transfers to recipients in the CBR fraud database (from 25 Jul 2024); a bank that fails refunds within 30 days. H
- Droppers: 176-FZ, Art. 187 parts 3–6 УК РФ, in force 5 Jul 2025 — handing over your card/account for gain: up to 3 years; buying/using others' payment means: up to 6 years; first-time offenders who help the investigation can be freed. Cash couriers: accomplices to fraud, Art. 159. H
- CBR dropper database: cards and online banking may be blocked (must, if data came from the MVD); from 1 Sep 2025 ATM cash max 100 k ₽/month, no new cards. H
- Register of controlled persons (РКЛ) for foreigners without legal grounds to stay, from 5 Feb 2025: no new bank accounts, transfers/cash 30 k ₽/month. M
- RuID app (since 30 Jun 2025): entry notification for visa-free foreigners at least 72 h ahead, creates a Gosuslugi account; experiment extended to 31 Dec 2027. M ⚠ (compulsory or not is unclear — the game says "strongly recommended")
- Gosuslugi 2FA mandatory for new users from 1 Oct 2023 (SMS, authenticator app or biometrics). M
- Personal data: Art. 272.1 УК РФ (illegal use/transfer of personal data) from 11 Dec 2024 (421-FZ); tougher КоАП 13.11 fines from 30 May 2025 (420-FZ). M. 187-FZ (critical information infrastructure). FSTEC Orders 21 (2013) and 239 (2017). ГОСТ Р 56939-2024 (secure software development), in force 20 Dec 2024. H

## Scam scripts (2025–2026)
"Safe account" (the CBR: such accounts do not exist); FSB/CBR/police call chains; "your mobile contract expires"; "confirm the doctor's appointment / new OMS policy — say the code"; fake boss in Telegram (sometimes a cloned voice); relative in trouble with AI voice/video; fake courier "tell us the code"; marketplace/Avito prepayment and fake "safe deal" pages; fake rentals with a viewing fee; "vote for my niece" Telegram hijack; fake investments ≥30%/year; fake jobs ("walking courier", "card administrator") = droppers; fake QR codes; fake state compensation sites; malware "bank app update"/remote access/NFC relay.
Advice: hang up and call the number on the back of the card; never tell SMS codes, passwords or CVV; take 24 hours before any urgent transfer; police 102, emergency 112; set the self-bans and a trusted person.
