# Five-minute demo script

Goal: show that this is a living world, not a quiz. Open the game, play along with the steps, and say the lines in *italics*.

## 0:00 — Title screen (20 s)
*"V Russia is a free browser life simulator. Two game years, seven starting lives, over four thousand scenarios."*
Point at the «Patriot» Award text, the city picker and the 7 characters. Choose **Tajik Migrant**, city **Moscow**, type a name, press **Start**.

## 0:20 — The newcomer's first week (60 s)
- Read the intro, then **Paperwork** (key 4): *"Fifteen days to register for a Tajik citizen (seven for most nationalities) — the clock is real."* Choose the honest option.
- Point at the sidebar: *document clocks, the first-month checklist, legal strikes out of 3.*
- **Paperwork** again until the medical exam, then the patent. *"₽10,000 a month in Moscow from January 2026, and you must notify the Interior Ministry within two months — otherwise the patent is suspended. We checked this against public sources."*

## 1:20 — Online services and the map (60 s)
- Press **💻 Online services** → *Job application*: fill in the resume, work authorization (point out that you can lie), pick a slot.
- Click a place on the live map (e.g. the bank): *"Snow makes you lose your way; a good phone helps."* Show the animated scene and weather above.

## 2:20 — Life outside the paperwork (60 s)
- **Culture & community**: answer a quiz question. *"Merit points lead to the community «Patriot» Award."*
- **People & love**: show a storyline scene (Timur the roommate). *"Characters remember what you chose."*
- **Book a flight** ✈️: fill the airline site to the SMS code; show the e-ticket. *"Departure day has its own scenes: airport, passport control, return check."*

## 3:20 — Depth (60 s)
- Open **My profile & awards**: goals, achievements, standing for the Patriot Award.
- Mention the business system (register a business online, weekly accounts), the admission → exam sessions arc, adoption of a dog from the volunteers' channel, hospital and family decisions.

## 4:20 — Quality (40 s)
*"Over 75 automated tests, a stress test that plays every choice of every scenario, and bot players that tell us whether a careful newcomer can actually succeed: 90–100% of careful migrants and students reach citizenship in two years — but only if they finish the legal route first; careless play ends in debt or deportation, as it should."*
Show `docs/BALANCE.md` and `docs/ITERATION_REPORT.md`.

## Backup plan
If something misbehaves live, press **⟲ New game**; progress autosaves, and an exported save file (title screen) restores a prepared state.
